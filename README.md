# AI-Study-Work-Balance-Assistant-
AI-powered Study-Work Balance Assistant for students to manage internships, projects, studies, weekly schedules, reminders, and productivity tracking 

## 1. Introduction
The AI Study-Work Balance Assistant is a web application designed to help students manage their studies, internships, projects, and personal commitments efficiently.

## 2. Problem Statement
Students often struggle to balance academic activities, internship tasks, project deadlines, and personal responsibilities. Poor time management can lead to stress, missed deadlines, and reduced productivity.

## 3. Proposed Solution
This project provides a simple digital assistant that helps students organize their tasks, prioritize important activities, plan their weekly schedules, and monitor their productivity.

## 4. Objectives
- Improve student time management.
- Organize academic and internship tasks.
- Set task priorities and deadlines.
- Support weekly planning.
- Monitor daily productivity.
- Reduce the risk of missing deadlines.

## 5. Key Features
- Task creation and management.
- Task categorization.
- Deadline selection.
- High, medium, and low priority levels.
- Daily productivity rating.
- Weekly planning support.
- Future AI-powered schedule recommendations.
- Future automated reminders.

## 6. Technologies Used
- Python
- Streamlit
- GitHub

## 7. System Workflow
1. The student enters a task.
2. The student selects a category.
3. The student sets a deadline and priority.
4. The application displays task information.
5. The student records a productivity rating.
6. The application supports better planning and time management.

## 8. Expected Outcome
The application aims to help students manage their responsibilities, organize their schedules, improve productivity, and maintain a healthy balance between studies and personal life.

## 9. Future Enhancements
- AI-based personalized timetable generation.
- Automated deadline reminders.
- Task completion status tracking.
- Weekly productivity reports.
- Stress and workload tracking.
- Integration with calendar applications.

## 10. Conclusion
The AI Study-Work Balance Assistant provides a convenient way for students to organize their daily activities and manage their time effectively. Future AI enhancements can make the application more personalized and intelligent.
